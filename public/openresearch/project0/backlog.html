StreamVGGT (not the method)

StreamVGGT is a causal KV-cache transformer. The VGGT-Ω FPV demo is not that: it is the 1B weights with VGGT-Long-style overlapping chunks. We stay on vggt_omega_1b_512.pt and change when frames enter model() (ego/vggt_omega_stream.py). Do not train or download StreamVGGT for Phase 0/1. 10B Omega is unreleased.

SAM 3 video tracking (Phase 4, not now)

Image PCS on independent frames is enough to judge in-view vs NOT IN VIEW. The SAM 3 video tracker / masklets belong with off-screen search (look around, update \(G_t\)). Do not run 300-frame tracking to pass Phase 1.

VGGT-Ω text alignment (not a grounder)

text_alignment_embedding is one L2-normalized vector per clip for scene-to-caption retrieval. Registers never see patches in that head. It cannot score “oven” on 3D points. Keep as a possible retrieval side channel, not the Phase 1 matcher.

Baseline D — CLIP into ActionPlan (not the method)

Code exists under ActionPlan-Code/: TRUMANS SMPL-X → 272, 73 teacher-forced windows with ego coverage, VGGT register cross-attn, train_actionplan_d.yaml, init from ActionPlan last.ckpt. Native Actions/*.txt are “pick up the vase”, not “walk to the table.”

Keep it as the “text embedding into the denoiser” baseline in a comparison table. Do not train it as phase 1. Do not grow the window set until grounding exists.

After phase 4

  • rc + PPO/Q on a frozen target: SMPL vs Scene_mesh, named object near and in FOV. Cheap depth/mesh in the loop. Do not terminate on collision. Randomize spawn and which object the text names.
  • Compositional language — “table next to the sofa”, “table behind you.” Output \(p(g, o \mid L, I)\) over instances, not a single blob.
  • Affordances — “sit at” / “look at” / “walk around” change \(G(\mathbf{x})\). First paper stays “walk to X.”
  • Held-out object names (train chair/table, test desk/cabinet) once in-view grounding is solid.

Do not do

  • Scale HML3D SE(2) placement. The smoke numbers were a diagnostic of room tightness.
  • SceMoS BEV as an input.
  • Fine-tune VGGT-Ω as a language grounder. The released alignment head is scene retrieval.
  • Train a motion model from scratch on 15 h of TRUMANS.
  • Expect 272 to keep hand–object contact.
